Welcome to Paradise

Sri Lanka is a tear-drop shaped island rich with endless golden beaches, timeless ruins, warm hospitality, misty mountains, and hill country. The smiles and hospitality of Sri Lanka is world famous, as are its spicy food, exotic fruits, and array of sweetmeats found nowhere else in the world. Experience breathtaking sceneries, mighty elephants, stealthy leopards, giant whales, and much more on this magical island.
